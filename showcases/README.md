# Liatir Scientific Showcases

Scientific studies carried out with Liatir, with protocols, measured results,
limitations and reproducibility evidence. The public
[Showcases documentation](https://liatir.com/showcases/overview) presents the
studies; each directory here is its canonical technical package.

## Studies

| Study | Scope | Technical package | Large artifacts |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Single-cell foundation models vs established baselines](https://liatir.com/showcases/single-cell-foundation-benchmark) | Two datasets; ten completed configurations and two documented UCE blockers. No uniform pretrained advantage was observed. | [Protocol, results, figures and evidence](single-cell-foundation-benchmark/README.md) | [Zenodo DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23187931) |

Source, small results, figures and validation records belong in Git. Large data,
embeddings and complete reproducibility archives belong in the study's external
artifact record. Each package states what its archive contains, what was actually
verified, and which failures or limitations remain.
