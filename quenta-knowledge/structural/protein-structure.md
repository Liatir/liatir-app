# Protein structure predictions

A predicted structure is a model with its own confidence and assumptions, not an observation. Read it
alongside the signals that tell you how much to trust it.

## How to read a predicted structure

Predicted protein structures should be interpreted with model confidence, sequence coverage,
oligomeric state, ligands, disorder, templates, and experimental context.

## A plausible rendering is not validation

A plausible three-dimensional rendering is not validation. Visual coherence says nothing by itself
about correctness.

## Binding and affinity predictions

Binding or affinity predictions require their own uncertainty and assay context. They are separate
claims from the structure and carry their own limitations.
