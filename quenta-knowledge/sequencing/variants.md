# Variant analysis

A variant call is the product of many upstream choices. The same sample can yield different calls
under different callers, filters, and references.

## What variant results depend on

Variant results depend on reference build, caller, filtering, ploidy, sample design, depth, allele
balance, mapping quality, strand bias, and normalization.

## Annotation is prediction, not diagnosis

Annotation such as SnpEff predicts consequences relative to a genome database and transcript set;
predicted impact labels are not clinical classifications.
